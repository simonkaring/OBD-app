# VoltLink icon studies

Four marks were drawn while settling the app icon. One of them ships; the other
three are kept as a record of the search. Each file is a single full-bleed
1024 × 1024 SVG.

| file | concept | outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`plate.svg`](plate.svg) | Off-white display plate overprinted by a mint twin, with the bolt knocked out of both so the dark ground (`#0B1012`) shows through. | **Shipped.** |
| [`tyres.svg`](tyres.svg) | Four tyres in plan view with a mint energy core and spark between them; no car body drawn. | Not chosen. |
| [`car.svg`](car.svg) | Side-view fastback silhouette with a bolt seated in a cut through the body — the second, lower and longer pass. | Not chosen. |
| [`comet.svg`](comet.svg) | Open comet-weight dial around a lime bolt on a deep green-black tile. | Not chosen. |

## Where the mark actually lives

The apps do not load these files. The shipped vectors are:

- **iOS** — `ios/App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon-1024.png`,
  `BrandMark.imageset/BrandMark.pdf` and `BrandMarkTemplate.imageset`.
- **Android** — `android/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml`,
  `ic_launcher_monochrome.xml`, `ic_brand_mark.xml`, `ic_notification.xml` and
  `ic_widget_mark.xml`, plus `ic_launcher_background` (`#0B1012`).

## What used to be here

This folder replaces the per-concept working directories `app-icon/`,
`app-icon-b/` and `app-icon-c/`. Those held, for every concept, the Android
adaptive foreground/background and monochrome layers, the iOS/Android PNG
exports, palette variants, preview sheets and the Python render scripts. On
consolidation only one master per concept was kept, so those derivations are no
longer in the working tree.

That means the platform layers of the three unchosen concepts would have to be
re-derived from the masters (foreground inset for the 66 dp safe circle,
monochrome silhouette) rather than regenerated by script. The shipped plate
mark is unaffected: its Android vectors and iOS assets are already in the apps.
