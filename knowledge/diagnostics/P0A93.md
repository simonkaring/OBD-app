# Meaning

Inverter coolant temperature exceeded target threshold during high load operation.

# Symptoms

- Electric motor power derating
- Cooling fan running at maximum speed

# Possible Fixes

- Check coolant fluid level in EV thermal loop
- Inspect electric water pump operation
- Bleed air from inverter cooling system
